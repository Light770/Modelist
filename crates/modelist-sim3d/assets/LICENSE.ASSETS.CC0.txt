Modelist 3D Mannequin Assets — License Attribution & Provenance

The binary mesh template, skeletal skinning weights, and anthropometric morph
targets stored in `mannequin_cc0.bin` are derived exclusively from the graphical
assets of the MakeHuman project (`makehumancommunity/makehuman`):
  - `makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj` (`body` mesh group, `hm08` topology)
  - `makehuman/data/rigs/default_weights.mhw`
  - `makehuman/data/targets/{macrodetails,proportions,measure}/*.target`
